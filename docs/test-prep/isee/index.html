Overview

About the ISEE

The ISEE is the entrance exam for many private and independent schools. It tests verbal reasoning, quantitative reasoning, reading, math achievement and includes an essay.

Format

What's tested

Verbal Reasoning

Synonyms and sentence completion.

Quantitative Reasoning

Problem-solving and quantitative comparisons.

Reading & Math Achievement

Grade-level reading and math skills.

Essay

An unscored writing sample sent to schools.
