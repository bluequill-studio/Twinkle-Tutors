FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does online tutoring work?

Lessons are live and 1-on-1 over video, with an interactive whiteboard. Your child needs a laptop or tablet with a camera and a stable internet connection.

Who are your tutors?

Every tutor has a college degree and a background check, and is interviewed and trained before teaching. Many are certified teachers or subject specialists.

Do you follow my state's standards?

Yes. Tutors follow Common Core or your state's own standards (such as TEKS in Texas, B.E.S.T. in Florida or the Virginia SOLs), plus your child's class syllabus.

Is the free trial really free?

Yes. The first session is free, with no obligation and no payment details needed.

Which time zones do you cover?

All of them. We schedule lessons in your local time, from Eastern to Hawaii–Aleutian.

What if we need to cancel a session?

You can reschedule or cancel at no charge with at least 24 hours' notice. See our Refund Policy for details.
