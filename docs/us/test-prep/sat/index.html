Overview

About the SAT

The digital SAT is a college admissions test made by the College Board. It has two sections, Reading and Writing and Math, and each is adaptive. Scores range from 400 to 1600.

Format

What's tested

Reading & Writing

Two adaptive modules of short passages, each with one question.

Math

Two adaptive modules. A calculator is allowed throughout, and Desmos is built in.

Digital & adaptive

Taken on the Bluebook app. Module 2 adapts to how you did on module 1.

Score range

400–1600, with section scores of 200–800.
