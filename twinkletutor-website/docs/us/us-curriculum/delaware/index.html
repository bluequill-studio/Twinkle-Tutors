How schooling works in Delaware

Delaware students learn to the Delaware Content Standards (Common Core-based). Students in Grades 3–8 and in high school take the DeSSA (Smarter Balanced).

Our Delaware tutors know the standards, the question types and the testing calendar, so lessons support classwork, homework and tests.
