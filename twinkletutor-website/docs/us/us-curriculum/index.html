US Curriculum & Standards

Tutoring aligned with US standards

The US has no national curriculum. Each state sets its own standards, and most are based on Common Core and NGSS. Our tutors follow your state's standards and your child's class syllabus.

Overview

How the US curriculum works

Each state sets its own learning standards, and local school districts choose the curriculum and textbooks. Many states use the Common Core State Standards for Math and English Language Arts, and many use the Next Generation Science Standards (NGSS) or a version of them. Others, like Texas, Florida, Virginia and Indiana, use standards they wrote themselves.

  • Grade-level standards from Kindergarten to 12th Grade
  • Annual state tests in Grades 3–8 and once in high school
  • High school credits, End-of-Course exams and AP/IB courses
