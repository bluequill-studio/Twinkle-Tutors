Pricing FAQs

Is there a sign-up fee?

No. You only pay for sessions.

Can I switch plans?

Yes, you can switch plans at any time.

What does 'pay per semester' mean?

You prepay a semester of sessions at a lower rate per session. Unused sessions are covered by our Refund Policy.

Do you offer sibling discounts?

Yes. Contact us for family pricing for two or more students.

Can I use an HSA, 529 or ESA?

Some states' education savings accounts and scholarship programs cover tutoring. Contact us and we'll help with the paperwork.
